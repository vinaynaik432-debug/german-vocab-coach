wordfreq contains data extracted from Google Books Ngrams
(http://books.google.com/ngrams) and Google Books Syntactic Ngrams
(http://commondatastorage.googleapis.com/books/syntactic-ngrams/index.html).
Their terms allow graphs and data to be freely used for any purpose and
appreciate acknowledgement of Google Books Ngram Viewer and a link.

wordfreq contains data derived from these Creative Commons sources:
- Leeds Internet Corpus, University of Leeds Centre for Translation Studies:
  http://corpus.leeds.ac.uk/list.html
- Wikipedia: http://www.wikipedia.org
- ParaCrawl: https://paracrawl.eu

It contains data from OPUS OpenSubtitles 2018
(http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles.php), originating from the OpenSubtitles
project (http://www.opensubtitles.org/), used with attribution to OpenSubtitles.

It contains SUBTLEX-US, SUBTLEX-UK, SUBTLEX-CH, SUBTLEX-DE, and SUBTLEX-NL
data by Marc Brysbaert et al.:
http://crr.ugent.be/programs-data/subtitle-frequencies
SUBTLEX remains freely available data. Robyn Speer obtained permission from
Marc Brysbaert to distribute these lists for any purpose, not only academic
use, provided the authors are credited and their free availability remains clear.

SUBTLEX-DE reference: Brysbaert, M., Buchmeier, M., Conrad, M., Jacobs, A. M.,
Bölte, J., & Böhl, A. (2011). The word frequency effect: A review of recent
developments and implications for the choice of frequency estimates in German.
Experimental Psychology, 58, 412–424.

Some wordfreq statistics were collected by a custom application watching
Twitter's streaming API under its developer agreement and policy. wordfreq
does not display or republish Twitter content and contains none from after
Twitter's sale.

This notice identifies the upstream sources; no raw books, subtitles, tweets,
or other corpus texts are included in this app's subset. Preserve this notice,
the embedded credits, source link, and data license when redistributing it.
