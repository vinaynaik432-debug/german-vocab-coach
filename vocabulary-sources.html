Words you can use, in a useful order.

Marketing first is the default. Switch the focus on Today to Everyday German or All my words. Continuous practice saves each answer separately; there is no compulsory round to finish. In Words, you can practise the displayed search results or an explicitly selected set.

Two different kinds of priority

Marketing: 120 words and phrases selected for CRM, campaign work, reporting, meetings, creative production, and research. Each area moves from essential terms to more specialist terms and useful phrases. This order is an editorial syllabus for your work; it is not a statistical ranking of German marketing conversations.

Everyday German: new words are ordered using German frequency estimates from wordfreq 3.1.1, by Robyn Speer. It combines sources such as subtitles, books, news, web text, and online discussion. Common words appear before rarer ones within the eligible word bank.

Due reviews come first within your selected practice scope. Learning and new words remain available; optional repetition is a deliberate choice. You do not need to finish every common word perfectly before encountering another word. Specialist marketing vocabulary remains available even when its estimated level is higher than your general level.

What the numbers can and cannot mean

Frequency depends on the texts being counted. A word that is rare in general German can be essential in your job. Frequency is also different from an A1–B2 learning level. Level labels here are teaching estimates, not official certification.

We look up the written word after removing a leading article or sich. This does not add up all its conjugations or distinguish every meaning. Phrases and hyphenated terms do not receive a pretend single-word frequency. Missing data means “no estimate,” not “nobody says this.”

The frequency snapshot is bundled with the app and works offline. It is based on the wordfreq dataset, not a live measurement of German in 2026.

What “recall verified” means

Recall verification requires independent recall across days. A word earns this label after three fully correct, unaided typed recalls on distinct local days, with at least 24 hours between qualifying recalls. A wrong or half-correct unaided recall resets the qualifying evidence. Correcting it after seeing the answer does not qualify. This is a practice criterion, not a promise that you will never forget the word or a measure of speaking fluency.

Stories, sentence and tense activities, context drills, and multiple choice record exposure or recognition; they never award word mastery from an overall score. Older mastered labels move to Learning for recall verification, while historical activity counters remain. “Practise recall” replaces the manual “Mark mastered” action.

The app now schedules reviews using transparent 1/3/7/14/30-day defaults, with shorter review after mistakes or assisted answers. The schedule is separate from frequency and recall verification; these intervals are product defaults, not a universal optimum. Older words without a schedule are not falsely labelled overdue.

Keeping your learning record

In Settings → Your data, use Download full backup and Restore full backup for a complete JSON copy of words, profile, settings, answer history, drafts, activities, retained sessions and recall/review evidence. New exports use backup version 2; supported version 1 backups can still be imported. Restore previews the file and checks for newer saved changes before replacement. It first saves a safety copy separately in this browser; download that copy to keep it outside the browser. A browser-local safety copy is not an automatic file download.

The original saved-data key remains untouched during migration. Writes stop when another tab changes the relevant data or saving fails, and unresolved responses remain available for retry or recovery export. Explicit repair of damaged current data requires additional confirmation and first preserves its raw bytes in a separately labelled recovery file. An ordinary restore never silently replaces damaged or newer-format data. Backups and recovery copies have different formats and purposes.

CSV is for word-list import and export, not a full backup. Future saves no longer trim history to 200 sessions, but sessions already removed by older versions cannot be recovered from the remaining data. Backups provide manual transfer between browsers, not automatic sync.
